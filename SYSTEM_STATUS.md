## 🎓 Interview-Ready Explanations

### Design Choices

**Q: Why particle constellation background?**
**A**: Creates a living ecosystem metaphor. Particles represent carbon molecules connecting in an organic network, making abstract data tangible and engaging. The gravitational cursor interaction gives users a sense of agency and connection.

**Q: Why neomorphic design?**
**A**: Neomorphism provides depth and tactility without harsh borders, making the interface feel more organic and natural. The soft shadows create a clay-like surface that aligns with our earth/sustainability theme.

**Q: Why biometric animations?**
**A**: Breathing and heartbeat rhythms create emotional resonance. They humanize the data, making climate action feel personal rather than abstract. The 4-second breathing cycle matches natural human breathing, creating subconscious comfort.

**Q: What about performance?**
**A**: All animations are hardware-accelerated using CSS transforms and Canvas API. We prioritize CSS-first animations, use Intersection Observer for lazy loading, and provide reduced-motion support for accessibility.

### Technical Implementation

**Q: How did you optimize the particle system?**
**A**: Canvas API for rendering, requestAnimationFrame for smooth 60 FPS, particle pooling to avoid garbage collection, and debounced mouse tracking to minimize calculations.

**Q: How do you ensure accessibility?**
**A**: Full `prefers-reduced-motion` support that disables all animations, high contrast mode adaptations, semantic HTML, keyboard navigation, and screen reader descriptions.
